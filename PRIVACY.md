# PhotoSwipe Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

PhotoSwipe does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **Your photos stay on your device.** PhotoSwipe reads your photo library through Apple's
  Photos framework only to show your photos and videos for review. Nothing is uploaded or
  sent anywhere.
- **Changes happen only when you confirm them.** Deleting or hiding photos happens only
  after you commit on the Review screen and confirm the system prompt. Deleted items go to
  Recently Deleted, where they stay for 30 days.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.** The iOS app makes no network
  requests. The Mac app's "Check for Updates…" contacts GitHub's public releases API to
  check for a newer version; it sends no personal information.
- **Local session data.** Your in-progress swipe session (which photos you've queued) is
  saved on your device so you can resume later. It never leaves the device.
